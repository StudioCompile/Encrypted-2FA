What happens to your data

Encrypted before it's saved

Everything is sealed with AES-256-GCM. The key is stretched from your password with two chained rounds of PBKDF2, 1,000,000 iterations in total.

Stored only in this browser

Vaults live in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, and you can export an encrypted backup file whenever you like.

Locks itself

After 5 idle minutes, 2 minutes in a background tab, or as soon as you leave the page, the key is wiped from memory.

Unlock your way

Use a password, a device passkey (fingerprint or Face ID), or both.

2FA codes

Scan a QR code, paste an otpauth link or type a secret. Codes refresh on the interval each service expects.

Files and notes

Store notes and files up to 5 MB. Add an extra passcode to any item for a second lock.

Sharing

Send an encrypted copy of a vault by link. The person you send it to still needs your password.

Vault runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
