Questions

Can you read my data?

No. Everything is encrypted in your browser with a key derived from your password. The server only stores ciphertext and can’t decrypt it. Read how it works.

What if I forget my password?

Use the recovery key you saved when you created the account. Without the password or the recovery key, nobody can decrypt your data, including us.

Does it work offline?

Local mode does. Once the page has loaded, codes are generated on your device, and local data never needs a connection.

Can I move my codes from another app?

Yes. Scan the export QR code from Google Authenticator, or import a file of otpauth:// links, from Settings.
