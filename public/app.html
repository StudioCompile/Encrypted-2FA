Make a passcode

You’ll type this each time you open Vault.

At least 10 characters. Try 3 or 4 random words.
We can’t reset your passcode for you. On the next screen you’ll get a recovery key. Keep it somewhere safe.

Unlock faster?

Use your fingerprint, face or security key instead of typing. You can skip this.

You can change this later in Settings.

Save your recovery key

If you forget your passcode, this is the only way back in. Save it somewhere safe. You won’t see it again.

Reset your passcode

Enter the recovery key you saved when you made your vault, then choose a new passcode. Your passkey will be removed, and you can add it again afterwards.
