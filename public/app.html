Create a passcode

This protects your vault. You’ll enter it every time you open Vault, and it’s never saved on this device.

At least 10 characters. A few random words is a good passcode. A short PIN can be guessed by anyone who copies your files, so add a passkey on the next step for hardware protection.
We can’t reset your passcode for you. You’ll get a recovery key next. Keep it somewhere safe.

Add a passkey

Unlock with Touch ID, Windows Hello, your phone or a security key instead of typing. Your passkey’s secret never leaves your authenticator, so someone who copies your files or has remote access to this computer can’t use it.

You can add or remove a passkey later in Settings. Your passcode keeps working either way.

Save your recovery key

If you forget your passcode, this key (plus your Google sign-in) is the only way back into your data. Vault can’t see it or reset it for you.

Reset your passcode

Enter the recovery key you saved when you set up your vault. Any passkey will be removed, and you can add one again afterwards.
